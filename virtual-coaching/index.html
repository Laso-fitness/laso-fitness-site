Common questions

Your training, made practical.

Do I need a gym or specialized equipment?

No gym is required for the introductory workout. A phone, tablet, or computer with a camera, some open space, and basic equipment if available are a good starting point. Requirements are tailored to you.

Can I train outside the United States?

Yes, virtual coaching is designed to work internationally for English-speaking clients, subject to mutually workable time zones and scheduling availability.

Do I need a LasO training device?

No. The virtual coaching introduction does not require you to own a LasO device. The training is centered on your goals and movement.

What happens during my introductory workout?

You'll discuss goals and exercise background, work through a coached training session suited to your level, and determine whether ongoing training is a good match.

Is the AI analyzing or recording every session?

Not necessarily. Technology-supported observation depends on the tools and setup available for a session. Any use of recording or retention should be explained and agreed to separately. Human coaching remains central.
