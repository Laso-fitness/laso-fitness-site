PRIVACY & CONSENT / DRAFT

YOUR MOVEMENT.
YOUR PERMISSION.

LasO's research tools should process participant footage only for authorized purposes and with informed consent. The public website does not ask visitors to upload movement videos.

Separate public and research systems

Marketing pages should not expose client video files, proprietary annotations or internal analysis logic.

Consent controls

Video recording, research use, cloud storage, and any future model training need distinct, understandable permissions. A coach's ability to access a file is not a substitute for participant permission.

Truthful measurement

Camera-estimated movement is different from measured external forces and should be labeled accordingly.

Website inquiries

Early-access and waitlist links on this preview lead to existing LasO destinations with their own data collection forms. Review their notices and retention policies before a public launch.

Important: This is an internal preview of privacy messaging, not a comprehensive legal policy. Before a public launch, the owner should publish verified contact information, data controller identity, collection and retention details, user rights and deletion procedures, vendor disclosures, and applicable legal notices.
