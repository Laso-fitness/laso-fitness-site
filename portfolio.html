01 / LASO MOVEMENT ENGINEWorking pilots + research

Context around the movement.

The problem: a repetition count alone leaves out timing, movement quality and the person’s circumstances.

My contribution: specify what the system should observe, what the coach should verify, when it should ask a question, and how uncertainty should be communicated. Review overlays and client controls, identify failure cases and direct corrections.

Current work: camera-based movement displays and coaching workflows in pilot applications. Temporal consistency, exercise-cycle analysis and sensor-assisted tracking remain research and evaluation topics.

A working overlay is not proof of biomechanical accuracy. This portfolio makes no clinical or direct muscle-force claim.

02 / APP-E-TIGHTWorking pilot

A food journal that asks first.

The problem: food logging becomes difficult when portions, routines and shared meals change.

My contribution: define a photo-to-review-to-save flow, make earlier entries easier to find, and shape optional accountability and shared-meal prompts. A missing entry should trigger a question, not an assumption that the person skipped a meal.

Current work: a separate nutrition-journal pilot with photo-based estimates and journal entry flows. Real-device checks and estimate validation continue.

Food-photo estimates require portion review and correction. The body-gauge concept is a product direction, not a validated physiological score.

03 / THE PHYSICAL LASOGranted patent

Resistance centered on the waist.

The foundation: a wearable steel-ball training system secured around the waist during use.

My contribution: inventor of the weighted belt system described in U.S. Patent 11,865,401 B2, granted January 9, 2024. Translate training use into physical product requirements and a connected product roadmap.

Future concepts: a sensor-ball add-on and an optional comfortable marker-band kit. Neither is presented here as a validated or shipping sensing product.
