Do I need any equipment? +
Two or three sets of dumbbells (light, medium, heavy) and a chair. That is it. No gym membership, no machines, no cables. The program is designed to work in any living room, garage, or hotel room in the world.
How does the app work? +
Open app-e-tight.netlify.app on your phone browser — no download required, no App Store, no waiting. It installs to your home screen and behaves like a native app. The Blue Marvel tab walks you through every week, every session, and every breathwork protocol with live timers and checkboxes.
What is the Blue Marvel breathwork protocol? +
It is a structured breathing system adapted from the Kox et al. 2014 peer-reviewed research on voluntary sympathetic nervous system activation. Phase 1 uses Box Breathing. Phase 2 introduces Power Breathing cycles. Phase 3 is the Full Blue Marvel Protocol with exhale holds. All protocols have animated timers in the app. The physiological sigh is used for post-session recovery. No equipment. No supplements. Just breathing.

Week 1 is accessible to anyone who can do a push-up. The program uses progressive overload — each week is slightly harder than the last, and every 4th week is a deload week. Whether you are returning to fitness after years off or are already athletic, the protocol adapts to your starting point.
